# Manager guide

## Deciding on leave requests
Approve or reject your direct reports' leave requests within 3 working days. Before you approve,
check that the team still has enough cover. If you reject a request, tell the person why. You can
only decide on the leave of people who report directly to you.

## Performance reviews
Write a review for each direct report twice a year: "H1" by 31 July and "H2" by 31 January.
Rate from 1 (far below expectations) to 5 (far above), and explain the rating in the comments.
Share the review with the person in a one-to-one meeting.
