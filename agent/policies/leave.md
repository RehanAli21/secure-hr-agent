# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid annual leave (holiday) per calendar year (1 January to
31 December). Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and must be taken
before 31 March. Ask for annual leave at least 2 weeks before it starts when you can.

## Sick leave
You get up to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Tell your manager on the first
morning you are ill. If you are ill for more than 3 days in a row, send a doctor's note to HR.
The reason for sick leave is private: only you and HR can see it.

## Unpaid leave
You can ask for up to 20 days of unpaid leave per calendar year, at least 2 weeks before it
starts. Unpaid leave is not counted as annual leave.

## Parental leave
Parental leave (maternity or paternity leave): the main carer of a new child gets 16 weeks of
paid parental leave, and the other parent gets 4 weeks. It must start within one year of the
birth or adoption. Tell HR at least 4 weeks before.

## Asking for and cancelling leave
Ask for leave in the chat or the app. A request is pending until your manager approves or rejects
it; nobody can approve their own leave. Two of your requests that are pending or approved can't
overlap. You can cancel a request while it is pending, or when it is approved but has not started
yet. Leave that has already started can only be changed by HR.
