# HR procedures

## Salary changes
Salary reviews happen once a year, effective 1 January. A raise of more than 10% needs the
approval of the Head of HR first. A new salary is always added as a new row: the salary history
is never overwritten. In the chat, a salary change is only proposed: it is done when you click
Confirm within 5 minutes.

## Termination procedure
Before terminating someone, check the last working day with their manager and with legal.
Terminating an employee disables their login. Pay the remaining annual leave with the last salary.
In the chat, a termination is only proposed: it is done when you click Confirm within 5 minutes.

## Role changes
Roles decide what someone may see and do in the HR system. Give the hr role only to HR staff, and
only admins can give or take away the admin role. Nobody can change their own role.
