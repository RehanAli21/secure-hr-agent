# Working hours policy

## Working hours
A full-time week is 40 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday: be available then.
You can choose when to start (between 7:00 and 10:00), as long as your team knows.

## Working from home
You can work from home up to 2 days a week, after agreeing the days with your manager. More than
2 days a week needs HR's approval. Public holidays are days off for everyone.

## Overtime
Overtime must be agreed with your manager before you do it. Overtime is given back as time off
within 3 months; it is not paid out.
